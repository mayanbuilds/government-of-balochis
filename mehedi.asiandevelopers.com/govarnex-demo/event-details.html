Event Overview

PDMA Balochistan organizes regular events to enhance disaster preparedness, community awareness, and inter-agency coordination across the province. These events include workshops, simulation exercises, and public awareness campaigns aimed at strengthening response capacity and promoting a culture of safety and resilience among citizens.
