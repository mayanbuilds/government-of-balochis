History

See History Timeline

1965

City Council
Founded

The City Council was established to strengthen local governance and ensure effective coordination in urban management and development. It serves as a bridge between the community and administrative authorities, focusing on planning, infrastructure, sanitation, and public safety.

1980

First Election
Conducted

The first city council election marked a major step toward strengthening local governance and public participation. Citizens exercised their right to vote and selected representatives dedicated to improving urban infrastructure, public welfare, and transparency.

2005

Drawing Around
City

Drawing Around” highlights the artistic expression and creative spirit of the people of Balochistan. Through vibrant sketches, murals, and local artwork, it reflects the region’s culture, resilience, and connection to its natural beauty. These creative efforts not only beautify public spaces but also inspire unity and pride within the community.

2015

Becoming a Smart
City

Balochistan is moving toward becoming a smart and sustainable province through innovation, digital transformation, and efficient resource management. PDMA and related departments are adopting modern technologies to improve disaster response, urban planning, and public services.
