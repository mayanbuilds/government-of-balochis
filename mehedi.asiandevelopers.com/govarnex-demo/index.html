Instant Services

Quick Services From The
PMDC

PDMA Balochistan ensures swift action during any emergency or natural disaster.” Our teams deliver rapid relief, rescue, and recovery to protect lives and restore stability.

  • Daily weather & disaster alerts
  • Coordination with rescue teams
  • Equipment and vehicle inspection
  • Relief warehouse maintenance
  • Staff safety drills
  • Public awareness sessions
  • Risk area data updates
  • Local emergency support
  • Coordination with NDMA offices
